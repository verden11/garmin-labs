# What real free/Pro pairs put behind the paid tier

Store API descriptions read 2026-09-28. Bucket ratios from `research_notes/Selling HeroSet and HeroFace/free_vs_paid_funnel.md`
(2026-09-22); ranges, never point conversion rates.

## MobileDriveway: GLANCE ladder (all faces)

| Listing | Price | Downloads bucket | Reviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| GLANCE watch face | free | 1,000,000 | 69,307 (4.9) |
| Glance Pro | **free** | 50,000 | 6,012 |
| Glance Ultra | 5,99 € | 10,000 | 1,683 |

Free listing's own words on upgrade: Pro "has added Open Weather support, TIME font weight setting, Body Battery, Stress Level,
more color options, full screen AOD option, and a lot more". Ultra "doubles number of glanceable metrics". Touch interface listed
Pro/Ultra-only. Free listing states "100% FREE", asks review in first paragraph, links four sibling app ids.
Attach GLANCE→Ultra: 0.2–5% (bucket arithmetic).

**Read:** ladder steps: (1) more data fields, (2) more colour options, (3) more layout/AOD options, (4) more of same.
Nothing in free face removed or degraded.

## GreenBlack: Fenix 8 V2/V3 (a hybrid, not our model)

- Free "Fenix8 V3 - GB": 100,000 downloads, 4,041 reviews, 4.9. "Available at no cost with all basic functions. Premium features
  (marked with [*]) require a Pro Key" sold on Ko-fi. Description also points to store PRO listing.
- Paid "Fenix 8 V3 PRO - GB": 5,99 €, 10,000 downloads, 2,000 reviews, 4.9. "Try before you buy!" first block, links free
  listing by store URL. "Send me an email and I'll send an unlock code": off-store fulfilment.
- V2 pair: free 100,000 → PRO 5,99 € at 50,000 (10–100% by bucket, best measured).

**Read:** even best-converting pair puts free-listing URL on first lines of paid page, vice versa. We copy that.
We do **not** copy Ko-fi key: off-store keys = largest monetisation complaint in review corpus (14.4%).

## What we take from it (feature ladder rules)

1. Free = "all basic functions", good enough for 4.5+ stars at volume.
2. Paid steps add data, colours, layouts, modes. Never remove fit, legibility, or time.
3. Free page names paid listing (with store URL) near top; paid page names free one.
4. Ask review in first paragraph of free page.
5. Publish more than one step only after first converts (GLANCE has three tiers after years).

## Excluded on purpose

TitanicTurtle's "VX" builds: trials with external payment, different mechanism than feature-tier twin. Their Elite listings
still show price band (3,49–4,69 €) paid faces reach at 10,000–100,000 downloads.
