# What real free/Pro pairs put behind the paid tier

Store API descriptions read 2026-09-28. Bucket ratios are from `research_notes/Selling HeroSet and HeroFace/free_vs_paid_funnel.md`
(2026-09-22) and are ranges, never point conversion rates.

## MobileDriveway: GLANCE ladder (all faces)

| Listing | Price | Downloads bucket | Reviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| GLANCE watch face | free | 1,000,000 | 69,307 (4.9) |
| Glance Pro | **free** | 50,000 | 6,012 |
| Glance Ultra | 5,99 € | 10,000 | 1,683 |

Free listing's own words about the upgrade: Pro "has added Open Weather support, TIME font weight setting, Body Battery, Stress Level,
more color options, full screen AOD option, and a lot more". Ultra "doubles number of glanceable metrics". Touch interface is listed as
Pro/Ultra-only. The free listing states "100% FREE", asks for a review in the first paragraph, and links four sibling app ids.
Attach GLANCE→Ultra: 0.2–5% (bucket arithmetic).

**Read:** the ladder's steps are (1) more data fields, (2) more colour options, (3) more layout/AOD options, (4) more of the same.
Nothing in the free face is removed or degraded.

## GreenBlack: Fenix 8 V2/V3 (a hybrid, not our model)

- Free "Fenix8 V3 - GB": 100,000 downloads, 4,041 reviews, 4.9. "Available at no cost with all basic functions. Premium features
  (marked with [*]) require a Pro Key" sold on Ko-fi. Its description also points to the store PRO listing.
- Paid "Fenix 8 V3 PRO - GB": 5,99 €, 10,000 downloads, 2,000 reviews, 4.9. "Try before you buy!" is the first block and links the free
  listing by store URL. "Send me an email and I'll send an unlock code": off-store fulfilment.
- V2 pair: free 100,000 → PRO 5,99 € at 50,000 (10–100% by bucket, the best measured).

**Read:** even the best-converting pair puts the free-listing URL on the first lines of the paid page and vice versa. We copy that.
We do **not** copy the Ko-fi key: off-store keys are the largest monetisation complaint in the review corpus (14.4%).

## What we take from it (feature ladder rules)

1. Free is "all basic functions", good enough to earn 4.5+ stars at volume.
2. Paid steps add data, colours, layouts and modes. They never remove fit, legibility, or time.
3. The free page names the paid listing (with the store URL) near the top; the paid page names the free one.
4. Ask for a review in the first paragraph of the free page.
5. Publish more than one step only after the first one converts (GLANCE has three tiers after years).

## Excluded on purpose

TitanicTurtle's "VX" builds: they are trials with external payment, a different mechanism than a feature-tier twin. Their Elite listings
still show the price band (3,49–4,69 €) that paid faces reach at 10,000–100,000 downloads.
