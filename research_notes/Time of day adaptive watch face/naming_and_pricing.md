# Time-of-day adaptive contextual watch face/widget — Naming and Pricing

Research snapshot: 2026-09-27. Name-collision checks used general web search (Google-indexed results, reliably surface individual `apps.garmin.com` listing pages when exist — confirmed by directly finding "DayShift" Connect IQ listing this way) plus targeted Connect IQ store page fetches. No trademark-register search (e.g. USPTO/EUIPO) run — "no collision found" below = no product, app, or trademark use turned up in general web and store search, not cleared legal trademark search.

## Brainstorm 5-10 candidate short app names in this studio's style, and report any collision found for each

### Takeaway
12 candidate names tested. 6 no product, app, or trademark use found anywhere (HeroDay, DayArc, Daybeat, DayCue, Daybreaker, Guideday); 6 collided with existing app or, in one case, existing Connect IQ store listing (Dayline, DayPulse, Daymark, Daywise, Dayrise, DayShift). **DayShift = direct in-store collision — existing free Connect IQ watch face by "johnnyw2" already uses exact name** — drop outright. Cleanest style fits among collision-free set: **HeroDay** (matches studio's existing "Hero-" prefix family used by HeroSet/HeroFace) and **DayArc** (evokes day's rhythm/arc like "TwoSuns" evokes two suns, not descriptive).

### Cited Findings
- **DayShift** existing, live Connect IQ Store watch face by developer "johnnyw2" (free, ~100+ downloads, 3.2★) — [DayShift | Connect IQ Store](https://apps.garmin.com/pl-PL/apps/34971426-f5ee-4e06-acf1-2ad6396f4f17). Direct in-store name collision; reject.
- **Dayline** collides heavily outside Garmin: "Dayline Watch Face" already exists for Wear OS ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.MOONApps.daylinewatchface)), plus at least four unrelated "Dayline"/"DayLine" apps (5-year diary, daily planner, habit/reflection journal) across iOS and Android — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dayline-tasks-calendar/id6751211696), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aporia.dayline&hl=en). Reject — collision especially strong because Wear-adjacent watch face already carries name.
- **DayPulse** existing task-management app (Apprizon LLC, iOS since October 2022; also Android), identical name — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/appstore/daypulse/ios-955035393). Reject.
- **Daymark** collides with at least five unrelated apps: daily journal, countdown app, habit tracker, mood tracker, "DayMark Wealth Partners" (financial-advisory app) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/daymark-daily-journal/id6760376611), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/daymark-date-countdown/id6751110336). Reject.
- **Daywise** existing, established habit-tracking app and, separately, existing Android notification-batching app of same name — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.appsur.daywise&hl=en_IN). Reject.
- **Dayrise** existing habit-tracker app with own marketing site — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habit-tracker-dayrise/id6749515833), [drise.app](https://drise.app/). Reject.
- **HeroDay** — no product, app, watch face, or trademark use found in general web search or Connect IQ-specific search. Fits studio's existing "Hero-" naming family (HeroSet, HeroFace) directly.
- **DayArc** — no product, app, watch face, or trademark use found in general web search or Connect IQ-specific search.
- **Daybeat** — no exact-name collision found; nearest neighbours unrelated "DayBand" fitness tracker and "Beat Watch Face" (LED-style face), neither exact match — [App Store: DayBand](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dayband/id1461078333).
- **DayCue** — no exact-name collision found; nearest neighbours "WatchCue" (where-to-watch-movies app, unrelated domain) and "Day One" (journaling app), neither exact match.
- **Daybreaker** — no product, app, watch face, or trademark use found in general web search.
- **Guideday** — no product, app, watch face, or trademark use found in general web search; weakest stylistic fit (reads generic/corporate, not evocative, unlike TwoSuns/DaysToGo).
- General trademark-risk context: Swiss court ruled certain watch-face apps violate **Swatch's trademark rights** over watch-face branding — live precedent that watch-face naming carries real trademark exposure beyond simple app-name collision — [swissinfo.ch](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/business/watch-face-apps-violate-swatch-trademark-rights-court-rules/49063424). None of candidates use Swatch- or Swiss-watch-brand-adjacent terms, but precedent supports owner doing real trademark search (flagged, unresolved, in prior TwoSuns naming research) before committing to any final name, not just repeating same web/store search done here.

### Inferences
- Studio's existing names (HeroSet, HeroFace, DaysToGo, TwoSuns) are either "Hero-" prefixed compounds or short evocative phrases saying nothing literal about feature set. HeroDay fits first pattern exactly; DayArc, Daybeat, DayCue, Daybreaker fit second (evocative of day's shape/rhythm without describing "adaptive guidance" literally), consistent with TwoSuns not describing "sun ring plus Body Battery curve."
- Names built on common self-improvement/productivity vocabulary ("-wise," "-line," "-mark," "-rise," "-pulse," "-shift" combined with "Day") heavily colonized by journaling, habit-tracking, productivity apps — six of six candidates in that lexical family collided. Names built on less habit-tracker-coded vocabulary ("-arc," "-beat," "-cue," "-breaker," "Hero-") came back clean across same search method, suggesting safer naming space for this product sits outside "daily productivity app" word-cloud entirely.
- DayShift existing free Connect IQ watch face with modest download count (100+) and low rating (3.2★): weak but real incumbent — not a wall against differently-styled app of same category, but exact-name collision disqualifying regardless of incumbent's own low traction.

### Gaps
- No formal trademark-register search (USPTO, EUIPO, or equivalent) performed for any candidate — matches caveat already recorded in prior TwoSuns naming research (`research_notes/Body Battery and sun face research/naming.md`), remains open for whichever name chosen.
- Search coverage of non-English app stores and non-English trademark use not attempted; name clean in English-language search could still collide in another market's storefront.
- General web search cannot browse Garmin's Connect IQ store search-by-keyword UI directly (WebFetch could not reach API endpoint independent of rendered listing page), so absence of hit inferred from Google's indexing of individual `apps.garmin.com/*/apps/{uuid}` pages, not from querying Garmin's own search index directly. DayShift hit = direct evidence this indexing surfaces real Connect IQ listings, raising confidence in other candidates' "no collision found," but not same as running query inside Garmin's own store search box.

## Survey free/paid pricing patterns for comparable multi-context Connect IQ faces/widgets

### Takeaway
Two prior local reports already established core pattern; do not re-derive: paid Connect IQ watch faces normal (51 of top-120 most-popular faces carry native Garmin price, band €2.49–€5.99) and configurability = single strongest correlate of success in top-100 popular faces (declared by 80% of top descriptions) — see `reports/Garmin watch face market gap.md`. Separately, paid "works for polish and breadth, not for a narrow single-idea face" — Circles 2 (broad, customisable dashboard) = one paid success story at 3.49€/10,000+ downloads/4.8★/1,716 reviews among Body-Battery-or-sun-themed faces, while every paid *narrow* single-idea face in that survey tops out at 2 examples reaching 1,000 downloads — see `reports/Body Battery and sun face research.md`. New evidence gathered here for *multi-context, time-of-day-adaptive* apps specifically (vs. sun/Body-Battery apps) reinforces same pattern, adds one strong direct precedent: **M2**, free, highly configurable Connect IQ watch face letting users define separate display "profiles" keyed to sunrise/sunset/dawn/dusk-relative time windows — exactly the "morning mode vs. evening mode" mechanic this product proposes — has 50,000+ downloads and 4.9★ rating, entirely free.

### Cited Findings
- **M2** (Connect IQ watch face, developer mark.ai): free, 50,000+ downloads, 4.9★. Users design multiple display "profiles", schedule them to load automatically at different times of day, start/end times settable relative to sunrise/sunset/dawn/dusk (e.g. "SUNRISE-00:30") — closest found precedent for Connect IQ product natively changing displayed content by time of day — [Connect IQ Store](https://apps.garmin.com/en-US/apps/1b4e3210-8105-487b-9f3a-8d538e6fed55); [Garmin Forums showcase thread](https://forums.garmin.com/developer/connect-iq/f/showcase/213203/watchface-m2).
- **Periodic Reminder** (Connect IQ widget, developer Trudelta): free, 1,000+ downloads, 2.8★ — single-purpose periodic-notification widget, closest found analog to "midday nudge" mechanic alone, without other contextual features bundled — [Connect IQ Store](https://apps.garmin.com/en-US/apps/31572290-9ef1-46fc-bb1b-245be3f5ee23).
- **Stress Meter** (Connect IQ widget, developer Trudelta): paid (exact price not retrievable from fetched page, listed as purchase not free), 1,000+ downloads, 4.8★ — single-purpose stress-display widget holding high rating despite paid and narrow, contrast to Periodic Reminder's free-but-2.8★ from same developer — [Connect IQ Store](https://apps.garmin.com/apps/d88d7ef0-7430-4968-b5e7-b4668696ee45).
- **Stress and relaxation cycles** (Connect IQ widget): free, 1,000+ downloads, 3.0★ — another single-purpose stress-context widget, mediocre rating — [Connect IQ Store](https://apps.garmin.com/en-US/apps/5c449a00-5643-4ab9-8b3c-715a19a0fc67).
- **SuperTask (Widget)** (Connect IQ widget, developer PedlarStudios): free, 1,000+ downloads, 2.3★ — general task-management widget; low rating despite free, consistent with broader corpus finding free alone does not buy good rating. — [Connect IQ Store](https://apps.garmin.com/en-US/apps/9a0c6082-3216-4573-b800-58a21dba97c5).
- From adjacent (non-Garmin) research already filed by parallel researcher in same folder (`competitor_landscape.md`), directly relevant to morning "dress-for-weather" feature: **Weather Fit: What to Wear** (iOS/Apple Watch complication) free with in-app purchases at **$3.99/month, $19.99/year, or $99.99 lifetime**, 4.6★ from 3.8K ratings — only found named, priced example of "dress for weather" guidance on any wearable platform, uses subscription/lifetime-tier model, not single Connect IQ-style flat price — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/weather-fit-what-to-wear/id1194408342).
- Same file: **Countdown!** (Connect IQ, event-countdown face) free, 4.1★, 100,000+ downloads; **GLANCE** (Connect IQ, unrelated to this project, clean/legible dashboard face) free, 4.9★, 1,000,000+ downloads — both free, both far larger than any paid narrow face found in either this survey or prior Body Battery/sun survey — [Connect IQ Store: Countdown!](https://apps.garmin.com/en-US/apps/183a7d45-c05e-4964-ba16-8f238eab2ba6), [Connect IQ Store: GLANCE](https://apps.garmin.com/apps/07ae0f49-7240-4475-a229-4507e8035fae).

### Inferences
- Across every multi-context or configurable Connect IQ example found (M2, GLANCE, Countdown!, top-120 ranking already documented in `Garmin watch face market gap.md`), highest download counts belong to **free** products; one paid success story at real scale in either survey (Circles 2, prior Body Battery/sun report) broad and customisable, not narrow. Now repeated pattern across three independent surveys (general market-gap, Body-Battery/sun, this time-of-day survey), raising confidence **genuinely multi-context, time-of-day-adaptive product = stronger candidate for "configurable, broad, defensibly free-or-low-priced" bucket than premium single price point**, echoing already-recorded owner decision to price TwoSuns and Days To Go at lowest paid tier, not higher.
- Stress Meter vs. Periodic Reminder contrast (same developer, one paid-and-4.8★, one free-and-2.8★): small-sample but clean illustration **price not what separates well-received widget from poorly-received one on Connect IQ; execution is** — directly consistent with market-gap report finding ratings cluster high across board, review volume/complaint-theme = real discriminators, not price.
- No Connect IQ example found bundling all three proposed contexts (dress-for-weather, midday nudges, evening energy summary) in one listing, so no direct precedent to price against for full concept — closest comparables single-context widgets (Stress Meter, Periodic Reminder, Stress and relaxation cycles, all free–low-paid, 1,000+ download tier) and one multi-mode watch face (M2, free, 50,000+, time-of-day-profile mechanic but no health/stress/weather logic). Read together with parallel `competitor_landscape.md` finding no shipped product anywhere (Connect IQ, Wear OS, or Apple Watch OS-level) natively switches own content by time of day like this concept proposes — pricing question answered by analogy to adjacent categories, not true comparable.

### Gaps
- Exact price for Stress Meter could not be retrieved (fetched store page rendered payment indicator, not numeric price); manual store visit or logged-in fetch needed for figure.
- Review *counts* (vs. star ratings) could not be retrieved for M2, Periodic Reminder, Stress Meter, Stress and relaxation cycles, or SuperTask from fetched pages — only aggregate rating and download-bucket rendered. Matches same store-page-fetch limitation already noted in parallel `competitor_landscape.md` ("Garmin's store page doesn't expose review text via fetch").
- Whether M2's time-of-day profile-switching drives its 50,000+ downloads and 4.9★, vs. general configurability/aesthetic quality, could not be determined — no review text retrievable to attribute cause. Treat "M2 succeeds" as correlated with, not proven caused by, time-of-day mechanic.
- No Connect IQ or wearable-platform example bundling dress-for-weather + event/break nudges + evening energy summary in one paid or free listing found anywhere, so no direct price-band precedent for full combined concept — only for individual pieces (see Inferences).
