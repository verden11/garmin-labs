DayArc / DayArc Pro on Instinct E and Instinct 3 Solar (176 x 176 and 166 x 166, 1-bit): look approved 2026-10-03, built layout differs (see the note)

As built (ADR-015): the arc is the window gauge; night draws no gauge (as on the round faces); Pro shows its grid only when the stack has room. The real fonts are bigger than drawn here. The outline is the real visible mask (a circle about 98 px in radius). This mockup shows the approved look. Mockup at 3x. Scope: DayArc needs Connect IQ 4.2 (Complications), so only the three Connect IQ 6 Instinct products qualify: Instinct E 40 mm, Instinct E 45 mm and Instinct 3 Solar 45 mm. The Instinct 2 family (CIQ 3.4) is out of reach without dropping Complications, which is a separate project. White on black only: the hue per window, the Accent colour setting and the 14 per-type icon hues cannot show on a 1-bit display, so the hero is told apart by its icon and label (shape), never hue. The round window (top right, 62 px; 52 px on the E 40 mm) is the physical subscreen: the day arc that rings the bezel on round watches becomes a gauge in it (hairline track, thick fill = how far through the day). Date sits in the band left of it; the clock is the largest element, full width below. Pro's field grid shrinks to one row of two cells. Dashed red = keep-out. Glyph shapes are a stand-in for the Garmin fonts; nothing here is measured on a watch.
