HeroFace on Instinct 2 (176 x 176, 1-bit): look approved 2026-10-03, built layout differs (see the note)

As built (ADR-002): the real fonts are bigger than drawn here (the smallest is 23 px tall), so the face has no footer, no temperature and no seconds; the time and date share the band left of the window, the streak sits below the window and the three columns end above the bottom corners. The outline is the real visible mask (a circle about 98 px in radius). This mockup shows the look that was approved, not the built layout. Mockup at 3x. White on black only: no grey, no accent, no gold, no green (a 1-bit display has only black and white), so every state is already carried by shape or word, as the design's Never-Colour-Alone rule requires. The round window (top right, 62 px) is the physical subscreen: the bezel ring that shows the day's progress becomes a gauge in it. Streak and date sit in the band left of it; the time is the largest thing, full width below; three mission columns and a battery/heart footer under that. Mission bars are outlined tracks with solid fills; a finished column shows a drawn check. On the Instinct 2 family (CIQ 3.4, no Complications) the HeroSet link is unavailable, so only everyday mode (steps, intensity minutes, floors) shows. Dashed red = keep-out zone. Glyph shapes are a stand-in for the Garmin fonts; nothing here is measured on a watch.
