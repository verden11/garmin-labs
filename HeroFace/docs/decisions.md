# HeroFace decisions

Durable decisions, newest last. The earlier choices (minApiLevel, Everyday-first, price, translation) are the numbered "Decisions" list in [`plan.md`](archive/plan.md); they were made before this file existed and still govern unless an entry here says otherwise. Cross-project decisions that bind HeroFace live in HeroSet's [`../../HeroSet/docs/decisions.md`](../../HeroSet/docs/decisions.md): [ADR-044](../../HeroSet/docs/decisions.md#adr-044) (the private complication that carries HeroSet's progress to the face, and its fixed field order) and [ADR-045](../../HeroSet/docs/decisions.md#adr-045) (the tenth field, the user's own daily goal).

## ADR-001: Free + Pro ladder

**Status: Accepted (Active) 2026-10-04: the owner approved the Free + Pro ladder on 2026-10-04 (OD1 and OD2 in `../../reports/Free and Pro ladder.md`).** Written 2026-10-01 as Proposed. The ladder replaces plan.md decision 8 (paid at the lowest tier, USD 2.00, one price for the one app) and the day-45 price-flip rule is retired. The Pro price is **decided by ADR-004 (price: the $2.50 tier for every paid app)**; still the owner's, not decided here: icons, translations and every upload. Nothing here is uploaded or priced.

**Decision.** HeroFace ships as a pair built from one codebase at compile time, the method DaysToGo and DayArc use (`../../DaysToGo/docs/decisions.md`, ADR-014 there, and `../../DayArc/docs/decisions.md`, ADR-003 there: no runtime toggle, no unlock key, no locked items):

- **Pro** is the existing app: `manifest.xml`, the live app id `8cd8f7f5-2169-42a6-b6c2-c1e797e2f313` (never changes), `monkey.jungle`, which excludes every `(:free)` symbol and uses `resources;resources-pro`. On-watch name "HeroFace Pro" (a placeholder: the owner names it). Version 1.1.0 (UNRELEASED). **Behaviour is intended to be identical to 1.0.1** (simulator suite passes: 24 tests on fr965, fenix5s and fr55, 2026-10-01; not checked on a wrist): the same seven settings, ids and defaults, and `resources-pro/settings` is byte-identical to the old `resources/settings` apart from the properties file's comment line.
- **Free** is new: `manifest.free.xml`, app id `be68898f-995b-45d9-860e-42ad508bd7fd` (generated with `uuidgen` 2026-10-01, never changes once published), `monkey.free.jungle`, which excludes every `(:pro)` symbol and uses `resources;resources-free`. On-watch name "HeroFace" (a placeholder). Version 1.0.0 (UNRELEASED). Same 117 products, same `minApiLevel` 3.0.0, same 15 languages, and the same single permission as Pro, `ComplicationSubscriber`. Free's permissions are a subset of Pro's, which here means equal: HeroSet mode is the point of Free (the funnel to HeroSet, plan WP6) and needs the permission, so Free cannot have fewer.
- **The split** (plan WP6 step 1). Free: Everyday mode and HeroSet mode (the Mode setting, Auto or Everyday), the slots fixed to Auto, Accent ids 0 to 2. Pro adds: the metric per slot (Slot1 to Slot3), Seconds, and Weather (the temperature). A Pro-only feature that needs an API Free does not need is not compiled into Free, so Free has no `Toybox.Weather` reads and no partial-update path.
- **Free shows no temperature and no seconds.** The temperature sits in the row under the time beside the streak; in Free that row carries the streak alone, centred, which the layout already did whenever the temperature was off. Seconds are drawn beside the time; in Free that box is never drawn.
- **Accents: HeroFace keeps its shipped three in both tiers (Blue 0, Cyan 1, Magenta 2); no colour is added.** The plan contradicts itself here. WP1 says HeroFace "stays at its shipped three unless the daring pass re-cuts its role palette or track" and gives it no ids above 2; WP6 step 1 lists Free accents "{0,3,4,5,6,7}" and Pro "0 to 11" and an "alternate layout". The WP6 line is DaysToGo's id table pasted in (ids 3 to 7 are Pink, Violet, White, Cyan, Lime there; White and Lime collide with HeroFace's reserved white and green, and the table gives HeroFace no id 3 or above), so it is outdated; this ADR follows WP1. Free and Pro offer the same three. The alternate layout does not exist and is not built (no redesign). Both are the owner's to settle, with the daring pass.
- **Magenta missed the track rule: resolved 2026-10-04 by ADR-003.** Shipped Magenta measured 2.84:1 against TRACK, under the face's own rule of 3:1. The owner chose to recolour id 2 (same id, same name), so Magenta is now `#FFAAFF` at 4.42:1; see ADR-003. (As first written, this entry left it open and `magentaMissesTheTrackRuleKnownIssue` pinned the failing figure.)
- **Settings files live only in tier folders**: `resources-pro/settings/` (every key) and `resources-free/settings/` (Mode and Accent). The shared `resources/` holds no settings file, so two never overlap and the compiler is never asked which one wins. HeroFace has no generator and the two lists are short, so both are written by hand; `tools/check_free_package.sh` fails if Free's Mode or Accent list differs from Pro's, which is the drift check a generator would give.
- **AppName** lives only in `resources-pro/strings` and `resources-free/strings`, not in `resources/` and not in the 14 `resources-<lang>/` folders: a language folder that defined it would override the tier name on a non-English watch. A language does not inherit the default's strings (the compiler warns "String id 'AppName' undefined for language ..."), so each jungle also appends its tier folder to every `base.lang.<l>` path. `tools/check_strings.py` enforces both rules.
- **Code** (all behind `(:pro)` / `(:free)` twins, the Free twin returning the default): `HeroFaceSettings` reads Slot1 to Slot3, Seconds and Weather only in Pro (Free: Auto slots, no seconds, no temperature, and no key requested); `HeroFaceReadings.temperature` (the Weather reads); `HeroFaceView.onPartialUpdate`, `disableSeconds`, the seconds-box field and `HeroFaceDelegate.onPowerBudgetExceeded`; and the `SETTING_SLOTS`, `SETTING_SECONDS` and `SETTING_WEATHER` constants. Leaving `onPartialUpdate` out of Free matters beyond size: the SDK 9.2.0 `WatchUi.WatchFace` reference says it "is called each second as long as the device power budget is not exceeded", so a face that defines it is called every second in low power and a Free face with no seconds should not be. Not removed from Free: the unreferenced display strings of the Pro settings (`setting_slot1`, `metric_*`, `setting_seconds`, `setting_weather`, shared with Pro, in every language) and the seconds drawing in `HeroFaceClock`, which only runs when a state carries seconds, so it is dead in Free; cutting it would fork the shared drawing and the screen-fit test's widest states. The Free build's settings, properties, on-watch name and compiled code carry no "Pro" word and no Pro key, and it has no locked or greyed item and no upgrade text.
- **Properties missing-key behaviour.** The SDK 9.2.0 reference (`doc/Toybox/Application/Properties.html`) says `Application.Properties.getValue` throws `Properties.InvalidKeyException` for a key absent from the properties file. Free never calls it for the Pro keys, so nothing depends on it; `HeroFaceSettings.read` already catches the exception. `freeMissingPropertyKeyThrows` records what the simulator does: it passed on the Free jungle (fr965, fenix5s, fr55, 2026-10-01), so the simulator throws `InvalidKeyException` for a missing key (not null, not a default). That says nothing about a real watch.
- **The HeroSet link is unchanged.** One private complication, field order fixed by [ADR-044](../../HeroSet/docs/decisions.md#adr-044) (the complication contract), same parser, same label, same `ComplicationSubscriber`. Nothing in `HeroFaceLink`, `HeroFaceContract` or HeroSet changes, so nothing in HeroSet's ADR-044 changes. **Open, owner and device:** the link is private, restricted to apps signed with the same developer key; the Free app has a new app id, signed with the same key. That it connects from a Free app id is not proven (simulator only, if at all); it needs the two apps on a watch.
- **Store text.** The Free description says in one line that HeroSet mode needs HeroSet installed (`listing-free/paste.md`). The existing `listing/` is not changed: a listing-repair submission is pending.
- **Verification** (`docs/development.md`, "Checking a store package"): `tools/check_free_package.sh` unpacks the compiled `.iq` packages and fails if the Free settings contain a Pro key, if "Pro" appears anywhere in the Free package, or if the Pro package lacks them; `tools/compile_sweep.sh` compiles every product for both jungles; `tools/run_tests.sh <device> [jungle]` runs the suite on either.

**Why.** Plan WP6 (`../../reports/Free and Pro ladder execution plan.md`): a free twin reaches the watches the paid listing cannot (Garmin sells paid apps only on its own list) and is the funnel to HeroSet, and the owner asked for the missing Free/Pro variants. A compile-time split keeps one source, no licence server and no locked UI.

**Consequences.** Existing paid users lose nothing: Pro keeps every setting and behaviour of 1.0.1. Free is a new, smaller app (no temperature, slots always Auto, no seconds). The live paid app's on-watch name becomes "HeroFace Pro" at 1.1.0: a visible change for existing buyers, the owner's call, and the plan folds the store rename into the pending listing-repair submission. The Pro headline is thin: three bars' metrics, seconds and the temperature are the whole difference, because the daring pass and the extra accents are not built. Typed-code cleanups needed to meet the strict compile (`-w --typecheck 3`) with no behaviour change: four casts or local variables in `HeroFaceLink`, `HeroFaceSettings` and `HeroFaceStreak`, and the same in three tests; the project had only ever been built at the default check level. **Owner decisions, not made here:** store names and titles, the Pro price, icons, translations of any new string, the uploads (Free 1.0.0 as a new app, Pro 1.1.0 on the existing id, together), the site deploy, the Magenta decision above, the third accent and the alternate layout, and OD1 to OD4.

## ADR-002: Instinct family: window gauge, black and white, no accent
**Status: Accepted (Active) 2026-10-04 (owner approved the ladder 2026-10-04). Written 2026-10-03 as Proposed; the owner approved the look (mockup `docs/archive/instinct-mockup.html`) the same day and chose to hide the Accent setting on these watches. Nothing has run on a watch (the owner has none): simulator evidence only. Not uploaded. Accepted does not mean device-proven.**

**Context.** `plan.md` listed the Instinct semi-octagon family as "phase 4, taken up only if the reviews ask". The owner asked for it on 2026-10-03. Seven products: `instinct2`, `instinct2s`, `instinct2x`, `descentg1` (CIQ 3.4) and `instincte40mm`, `instincte45mm`, `instinct3solar45mm` (CIQ 6.0). `instinctcrossover` is left out as in HeroSet ADR-055 (its analog hands cover the display; the simulator shows no window). They are 1-bit (palette `000000`/`FFFFFF` only), 176 x 176 (the E 40 mm 166 x 166, the 2S 163 x 156), with a round **window** (62 px, 52 px on the E 40 mm, 54 px on the 2S) cut into the top-right corner. **Watch-face memory is 65,536 B** (ADR-001's 96 KB floor does not hold).

**Decision.**
- **Products.** The 7 join both manifests (124 products in each tier), one build per tier. They are at or above `minApiLevel` 3.0.0.
- **Layout.** `HeroFaceLayout` asks `WatchUi.getSubscreen()` on `SCREEN_SHAPE_SEMI_OCTAGON` products only, so no round product's geometry depends on it. Rows are boxes: a side margin each side, and a row that starts above the window's lower edge plus a ring clearance ends left of it and is centred in the band that is left (`rowCenterX`). **The bezel ring becomes a gauge in the window** (hairline circle, thick fill from 12 o'clock, closed when every goal is done; the other ring never closes). As built: the time (the largest of `FONT_NUMBER_HOT`/`MEDIUM` that fits the band, 44 px on a 176 px screen) and the date share the band left of the window, the streak sits just below the window, and the three mission columns (value, bar, label) end above the bottom corners.
- **What is not drawn on an Instinct.** The footer (battery, heart rate, notifications), the temperature and the seconds: the watch's smallest font is 23 px tall on a 176 px screen (20 px on the 166), so they have no room. The mockup (`docs/instinct-mockup.html`, drawn with 15 px type) showed the footer and a bigger time; the real face is the layout above.
- **The visible area is a circle (found by simulator screenshot, 2026-10-03).** The bezel hides the corners: what shows is the square cut by a circle about 98 px in radius (96 to 100 px, from the alpha mask of the SDK's device images, all seven products). `HeroFaceLayout` clips every row's insets to a 96 px circle (`VISIBLE_RADIUS_PX`) and the fit test fails any text box whose corner leaves it (`collectCorners`; boxes include font padding, so this is stricter than the ink). The first built layout had its mission labels clipped by the corners ("STEPS" lost its S); the fix is what made the columns narrower and the labels use their short forms.
- **Black and white by annotation.** `HeroFacePalette` is two classes with the same name, `(:color)` and `(:mono)`; the jungles exclude `mono` for every product and `color` for the 7 Instinct products. All roles are white. The bar track is an outline under a solid fill; done is a full bar plus the drawn check (never green alone); a low battery has its number. **A per-product `excludeAnnotations` line replaces the base list**, so the tier's own exclude is restated (`free;color` in `monkey.jungle`, `pro;color` in `monkey.free.jungle`); writing `color` alone would silently break the Free/Pro split.
- **Accent hidden on Instinct (owner, 2026-10-03).** The Accent list is its own settings file (`resources-accent-<tier>/settings/accent.xml`); Pro's Seconds and Weather moved to `resources-pro-tail/settings/tail.xml` so the phone's order stays Mode, Slots, Accent, Seconds, Weather on every other product. The Instinct `resourcePath` leaves the accent folder out, so those watches show no Accent setting. The property stays in `properties.xml` (a shipped key never changes) and `HeroFacePalette.accent()` returns white. A second resource folder cannot remove a setting (the compiler merges settings files); a folder that is absent can.
- **HeroSet link.** On the Instinct 2 family (CIQ 3.4) there are no Complications, so the face stays in Everyday mode there (`Toybox has :Complications` is false in the simulator); Instinct E and Instinct 3 Solar (CIQ 6.0) can link to HeroSet, which now runs on them (HeroSet ADR-055). The complication field order (HeroSet ADR-044) is untouched.

**Consequences.** Pro on an Instinct has no seconds, no temperature and no footer; Free and Pro look the same there. No round product's drawing changed: `rowCenterX` returns the centre and the insets are unchanged without a window (round control suites pass).

**Site.** `../site/src/apps/heroface/facts.ts` still says `watchCount = 117` and "Every round watch", which stays true for the live app. Nothing about Instinct goes on the site until an upload is approved and the store lists the watches; then gate the copy behind a flag as HeroSet does (`instinctLive`) and update the count.

**Amendment 2026-10-04 (owner's standing authorisation to redesign HeroFace UI/UX; ROADMAP 9.10).** On the 176 px Instinct the three columns are about 42 px wide and "check + STEP" did not fit, so a finished step goal read "✓ ST." (a label cut with a "."). On a 1-bit display a finished goal's label is now **reversed**: black on a white pill two pixels wider each side (`HeroFaceMissions.drawLabel`, `HeroFaceLayout.doneLabelPad`), no check, label never cut for the check; the full solid bar still says "done" there as well. Colour products keep the green check and label unchanged. Evidence: simulator screenshots of Free and Pro on `instinct2` and `instincte40mm` (a 12-day streak seeded through the app's own streak code in a private copy, because the simulator's history rows did not apply: "13-DAY STREAK" and "12-DAY STREAK" drew whole). Simulator only, no watch.

**Verification.** See `docs/compatibility.md` "Instinct family". Simulator only.

## ADR-003: Magenta recoloured to pass the track rule
**Status: Accepted (Active) 2026-10-04. Owner decision 2026-10-04 (ROADMAP 5.3, built as 5.8): "fix the HeroFace Magenta accent colour so it passes 3:1."**

**Context.** The face's own accent rule is 3:1 against TRACK (`#555555`) so a part-filled bar still reads. Blue (`#55AAFF`, 3.05) and Cyan (`#00FFFF`, 5.95) pass; Magenta (`#FF55FF`, id 2) measured 2.84 and failed (ADR-001 recorded it as a known issue).

**Decision.** Id 2 keeps its id, its place in the list and its name "Magenta" in every language; its colour becomes `#FFAAFF`. That is the only 64-colour value that is still a magenta and clears the rule: every channel is 00, 55, AA or FF, and green must rise from 55 to AA to lift the luminance above 0.372 (the 3:1 line against TRACK); `#FF00FF` is 2.38, `#AA55FF` (violet) and `#FF55AA` (pink) are lower still. Measured: `#FFAAFF` is 4.42:1 on TRACK and 12.45:1 on black. It collides with no reserved role (gold `#FFAA00`, green `#00FF00`, alert red `#FF0000`, white `#FFFFFF`, MUTED `#AAAAAA`, TRACK; `noAccentIsAReservedRoleColour` passes). Its price is that it is a pale magenta, closer to the time's white (1.69:1 against white) than the old one (2.63). Ids stay append-only for everything else: Blue and Cyan never changed.

**Why a recolour of the shipped id, against the append-only rule.** The rule exists so a saved setting does not change meaning. Here the meaning (Magenta) is kept and only the shade moves, by the owner's explicit call; a new id would have left two Magentas in the list and the phone's stored `2` on the failing colour. Existing Pro users who chose Magenta see the lighter shade after 1.1.0: a visible change, listed in the CHANGELOG.

**Verification.** `everyAccentClearsTheTrackRule` (all three), `magentaWasRecolouredToClearTheTrackRule` (pins 4.42), `shippedAccentIdsKeepTheirColours` (Blue, Cyan and the new Magenta) and the full suite on fr965, fr255s, epix2, instincte40mm and instinct2, both tiers (the Instinct has no Accent setting and draws white); a simulator screenshot with Magenta selected. Simulator only; not looked at on a wrist.

## ADR-004: Price: the $2.50 tier for every paid app

**Status: Accepted 2026-10-04 (owner, chat).** Supersedes the price of plan.md decision 8 (paid at the lowest tier, USD 2.00; `archive/plan.md`) and resolves the "Pro price" left open by ADR-001 (the Free + Pro ladder).

**Decision.** HeroFace Pro (the paid app, live app id `8cd8f7f5-2169-42a6-b6c2-c1e797e2f313`) moves to the **USD 2.50 tier** of Garmin's price points (US $2.49, eurozone 2,99 EUR; measured table in `../../research_notes/Free and Pro ladder/garmin_rules.md`, source https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/monetization/price-points/). Every paid app in the studio (HeroSet, HeroFace Pro, Days To Go Pro, Two Suns Pro, DayArc Pro) takes the same tier. HeroFace (Free) stays free. The owner sets the tier in the upload form.

**Why.** Room for later discounts or a rise: Garmin's tiers are $2.00, then every $0.25, so the $2.00 tier left no step down. The tier converts to a different number in each store, so no number is stated where a reader sees it.

**No price number on the site or in listing text.** Neither listing's Description or What's New, nor the pages under `site/src/apps/heroface/`, state a price.

**Open risk.** Garmin documents that changing the price of an approved app can take it out of the store for re-review (SDK `Monetization/App_Sales`); how it treats a repricing to a higher tier is not confirmed. Ship the change together with the next version upload (Pro 1.1.0), which is re-reviewed anyway. The Garmin email on repricing was cancelled (owner, 2026-10-04, [`../../ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md) 2.1); the agent re-reads Garmin's published policies instead (`../../reports/Garmin policies and design guidelines.md`, running), so the risk stays open until that report answers it.

**Reversed by.** The owner.

## ADR-005: Rectangle family: the ring follows the screen's edges
**Status: Proposed 2026-10-05 (agent, on the owner's request to add the rectangular watches). Simulator only, nothing on a watch. The rectangle look needs the owner's look-approval before any upload.**

**Context.** `compatibility.md` left the rectangles out: "the row stack assumes a round chord; a rectangle wants its own proportions" (`archive/plan.md` phase 4). Five products at or above `minApiLevel` 3.0.0: `venusq`, `venusqm` (240 x 240 LCD, CIQ 3.3.6; `venusq` has the 96 KB watch-face budget), `venusq2`, `venusq2m` (320 x 360 AMOLED, CIQ 5.0) and `venux1` (448 x 486 AMOLED, CIQ 6.0, glass rounded about 53 px at the corners). With no code change the round ring sat on the inscribed circle, cut the mission row on `venusq2` ("MOVE" under the ring), and the time's box (81 px at `FONT_NUMBER_MILD`, the smallest number font there) did not fit its 71 px band, so `everyStateFitsThisDisplay` failed there.

**Decision.**
- **Products.** The 5 join both manifests (129 products in each tier, one build per tier, no per-device resources, no bitmaps).
- **The ring becomes a frame** (`HeroFaceLayout.rectangle()`, `frameBox`, `frameFillFor`; `HeroFaceRing.frame`): an open-bottom rounded rectangle, inset and as wide as the round ring (a fifth and a sixth of the inset), corner radius 1.5 insets (clears the X1's rounded glass; the corner, the margin and the time were reworked in the 2026-10-05 amendment below). The track runs up the left side, over the top and down the right side; the bottom edge is the open gap where the footer lives (the Open Ring Rule's rectangular form). The fill is measured along that path in pixels from the lower end of the left side, clockwise like the round ring's 220° origin; any progress shows a pixel, a full share fills the whole path, it never closes. Same colours and meaning: the day as a whole (accent, green when full), or XP into the rank in HeroSet mode (gold).
- **Rows fit inside the frame** the way round rows fit inside the ring: half the ring width plus half the text margin in from its centreline, and inside a rounded corner at all four corners (the X1's glass is rounded at the bottom too). That replaces the old constant "one inset" for non-round screens, which had no product until now. The top and bottom margins are half an inset (no chord to clear); that is what gives the time its band on `venusq2`.
- **The time keeps room for Pro's seconds on a rectangle.** The wide band would otherwise take `FONT_NUMBER_THAI_HOT` on the X1 with no room beside it; on a rectangle the time font is chosen with the seconds' width reserved on both sides (the time stays centred). Round products are unchanged.
- **Unchanged:** the round and Instinct paths (their insets and ring code are not reached on a rectangle), the stack order, the fonts, the always-on time (it still drifts on a 3 x 3 grid on the burn-in AMOLEDs), the complication contract (HeroSet ADR-044, the field order) and the Free + Pro split (ADR-001, the ladder).
- **HeroSet mode** needs `Toybox has :Complications`: true on `venusq2`, `venusq2m`, `venux1`; false on `venusq` and `venusqm` (CIQ 3.3), which stay in Everyday mode.
- **Paid vs free reach (ROADMAP 10.15 precedent, ADR-004 for the price).** Venu Sq 2, Sq 2 Music and Venu X1 are on Garmin's App Sales list (API 5.0 and 6.0 tiers) and are sold in the live paid listings of Two Suns Pro and DayArc Pro, so HeroFace Pro can be sold there. Venu Sq and Sq Music (CIQ 3.3.6) are not on the list (its lowest tier is API 3.4): both stay in both manifests, as the Instinct 2 family did, and only the Free twin reaches them. No model names in listing text.

**Consequences.** Test counts go up by one (`rectangleRingStaysOnTheDisplay`, which on a rectangle checks the frame stays on the display and its fill arithmetic, and elsewhere only that the product is not a rectangle): Pro 26 and Free 26 on round and rectangle products, 22 each on an Instinct.

**Verification.** `docs/compatibility.md` "Rectangle family". Simulator only.

**Amendment 2026-10-05 (square design pass; the owner asked for real square designs and chose to approve the built simulator screenshots before any upload).** Still Proposed, simulator only. The first build's screenshots showed a small time with a large empty band under it on `venux1` and `venusq2`, and a frame close to the glass on `venux1`.
- **The time is sized and placed by its digits' ink, not its font box.** A number font's box is about a third empty headroom and descent (digits fill 0.68 to 0.69 of the ascent, measured on simulator screenshots of all three sizes, not on a watch; the one stated exception to DESIGN.md's Measure-Never-Guess Rule, since the Dc has no glyph metrics; `HeroFaceFrame.DIGIT_HEIGHT_PERMILLE` 720, DayArc's figure). On `venusq2` the 103 px band missed `FONT_NUMBER_MEDIUM`'s 105 px box and fell to `MILD` (41 px digits). Now the largest number font whose ink leaves a stack gap above and below it in the band, and whose widest time fits the frame, is taken: `venusq` `THAI_HOT` (39 px digits, was `HOT` 28), `venusq2` `HOT` (80, was `MILD` 41), `venux1` `THAI_HOT` (108, was `HOT` 86). The screen-fit test checks the ink box for the time on a rectangle.
- **The stack is balanced.** The height the time does not use is shared evenly by the gap above the time, the gap under it and the gap above the footer (the missions and the row under the time move up together), so no hole sits under the time.
- **The seconds' room is reserved only while seconds are drawn.** The first build kept Pro's seconds' width free beside the time on every rectangle, in both tiers; Free has no seconds and paid for it. Now the layout keeps two time fonts (`timeFont`, `secondsTimeFont`, the same font on round and Instinct products) and the clock takes the second only while seconds are drawn: with seconds on, `venusq2` drops to `MEDIUM` and `venux1` to `HOT`, the time staying centred (centring time and seconds as one group would not keep the larger font either: 364 + about 40 px exceeds the X1's 398), and it grows back once if the watch cuts the seconds for the power budget. This is a stated exception to "don't shrink a font to make text fit"; the owner can overrule it in the look-approval.
- **The frame clears the glass with an even margin.** The centreline sits a quarter inset in (8 px of black between frame and glass at 448, was about 5), and each corner is a quarter circle centred 1.5 insets in from both edges. The Venu X1's glass corner measures about **68 px** off the SDK's device skin (60, 24, 10 and 0 px in from the side at 0, 16, 32 and 60 px down; the "about 53 px" above was wrong), 1.55 insets, so the frame runs nearly concentric with it and the margin stays about even round the corner. The Venu Sq and Sq 2 skins are almost square (about 7 px): there the round corner is the design's own, not a glass constraint.
- **The fill keeps its lower-left start (decision).** The studio rule for rectangles (a track that follows the screen, from top centre, clockwise, share = share of the path's length) describes a closed track. HeroFace's ring is open by design (DESIGN.md, the Open Ring Rule: the footer lives in the gap) and its round form starts at the lower left (220°, HeroSet's XP ring). Started at top centre, the open path would either split the fill across the footer gap (the right side fills, then the fill jumps to the lower left) or have to close and take the footer's room. So it keeps the round ring's origin and direction, and takes from the studio rule what applies to an open track: clockwise, and share = share of the path's length. A user who owns a round and a square HeroFace reads the same gesture on both.
- **The owner's steering "not a round design wrapped in a frame" (2026-10-05).** The frame is the ring's rectangular form, a track that follows the screen's edges (the studio rule), not a circle in a box; rows use the frame's full width, not an inscribed circle. Whether this reads as a square design is the owner's look-approval on the screenshots.
- **Code.** The rectangle geometry moved out of `HeroFaceLayout` into `HeroFaceFrame` (the frame, its fill, the row inset, the stack and the ink-based time font). Round and Instinct paths are unchanged (no frame is built there; their two time fonts are the same font). Test counts are unchanged: `rectangleRingStaysOnTheDisplay` also checks the corner's centre and that the time is never smaller with seconds off.
