# Verden site — app landing surfaces

Scope: studio home `/` and per-app landing `/<slug>/` (Persuade); per-app support + privacy (Read). Visitors: Garmin athletes arriving from the Connect IQ Store listing, plus store reviewers opening privacy/support URLs. Constraints: static, no JS needed to read anything; claims bounded by each app's release contract; no invented screenshots, reviews, prices or accuracy numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every app is an event in one sports program: one spectral color, its own geometric pictograms, one shared grid. Refuses the indie-studio default (icon + headline + badge + screenshot row on white).

OWN-WORLD: Munich-72-style identity program. Flat full-bleed color fields (HeroSet amber #FFAA00, studio silver/blue), near-black ink, white ground. Pictograms on a strict 45°/90° grid, thick cut limbs, detached round head. Archivo grotesque, huge tabular numerals vs small tracked caps labels; hierarchy by scale contrast alone (raise from Type Specimen). No cards-with-shadows, no gradients.

STORY: Visitor sees three pictograms doing reps toward 100, understands "daily 100 of each, counted on the watch", believes it because every claim is concrete (button names, what stays on watch, beta stated), then goes to store or support.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full amber field. Left 5 cols: HeroSet name huge, one-line offer, store action + support link. Right 7 cols: three pictograms in a row, each looping its two-pose rep with a tabular count ticking /100 in discrete steps (raise from Nixie Counter: a count is a visible event). Studio bar above.

FORM: Games identity program, #4 on the ordered list, seed key aa1b29e3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
