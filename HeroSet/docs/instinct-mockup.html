HeroSet on Instinct 2 (176 x 176, 1-bit): LOOK UNAPPROVED

Mockup at 3x. White on black only, no grey. The round window (top right, 62 px) is the physical subscreen: the dashboard uses it as the XP gauge. Mock row text is illustrative: the app picks wording and fonts by measuring. Dashed red outline = the keep-out zone text must stay out of (window plus about 10 px of bezel ring). Text widths are set from the Instinct 2 simulator's measured font widths (about 9.5 px per capital at the small text size); glyph shapes are a stand-in for the Garmin fonts, and nothing here is measured on a watch.
