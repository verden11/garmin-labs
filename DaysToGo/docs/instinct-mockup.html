Days To Go on Instinct 2 (176 x 176, 1-bit): look approved 2026-10-03, built layout differs (see the note)

As built: the hero is 44 px (the real fonts are bigger than drawn here; the smallest is 23 px tall), there is no footer and no accent, and the visible area is clipped to a circle about 98 px in radius (the bezel hides the corners; the outline below is the real mask). See ADR-015. Mockup at 3x. White on black only, no grey and no accent colour (a 1-bit display has only black and white). The round window (top right, 62 px) is the physical subscreen: the ring that drains around the bezel on every other watch becomes a gauge in the window (hairline track, thick fill). The time and event name sit in the band left of it; the hero number gets the full width below. The footer row is dropped (battery or steps, Pro only), as HeroSet drops its XP line. Dashed red = keep-out zone for text. Glyph shapes are a stand-in for the Garmin fonts; nothing here is measured on a watch. Row wording is illustrative: the app measures and picks fonts.
