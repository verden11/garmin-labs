Privacy policy

HeroSet for Garmin watches. Effective 17 September 2026.

In short: HeroSet keeps everything on your watch. It has no account, no internet access, no analytics and no ads, and it does not record or sync activities.

What the app reads on your watch

  • Accelerometer, while a set or calibration is running, to count reps. Readings are processed as they arrive and are not saved.
  • Heart rate, while a set is running, to show it on screen. It is not saved.
  • Garmin's daily calorie total, at the start of a set and while it runs, to show an estimate of calories burned. It is not saved.

The only permission HeroSet requests is access to the watch's sensors.

What the app stores on your watch

  • Today's push-up, sit-up and squat totals, and the date they belong to.
  • Your XP, streak and the date you last completed the daily goal.
  • Your calibration settings for each exercise (a few threshold numbers).
  • A short list of your last 30 sets: the date, the exercise, the count the watch detected and the count you saved. It helps improve counting accuracy and never leaves the watch.

This data stays in HeroSet's private storage on the watch. Deleting HeroSet from your watch deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer, to Garmin Connect, to Strava or to anyone else.
  • It does not create or save activities.
  • It does not use location, contacts, or your phone.
  • It does not include analytics, advertising or tracking.

Purchases

If you buy HeroSet, the purchase is handled by Garmin through the Connect IQ Store under Garmin's privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Children

HeroSet does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes what HeroSet stores or shares (for example, an optional Garmin Connect sync), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the store listing.
