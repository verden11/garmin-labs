HeroSet

A daily 100 push-ups, 100 sit-ups and 100 squats challenge for Garmin watches.

Contact

Getting accurate counts

  • Calibrate each exercise first. Main menu, Calibrate Exercise, pick the exercise, press START, then do 10 normal reps. The watch finishes by itself after the tenth.
  • Wear the watch snug, in the same spot as when you calibrated. Recalibrate if you switch wrists or move the strap.
  • Hold still for a second after pressing START, then begin your first rep.
  • Move through full reps. Tiny partial movements may not count.

Automatic counting is in beta and can miscount. When you finish a set, press START to review the count, adjust it with UP/DOWN, then press START again to save.

Common questions

Does HeroSet add activities to Garmin Connect or Strava?
No. HeroSet records no activity and sends nothing to your phone or the internet. Your progress lives on the watch.

Are the calories exact?
No. The calorie figure is the change in Garmin's own daily calorie total during your set, an estimate. HeroSet is not a medical device.

When do the daily counts reset?
At midnight, watch local time. XP, rank and streak carry over; missing a day resets the streak.

Which watches are supported?
Garmin watches with five buttons and a round screen: Forerunner 70, 165, 170, 255, 265, 570, 945 LTE, 955, 965 and 970; epix (Gen 2) and epix Pro (Gen 2); fēnix 6, 6 Pro, 7, 7 Pro, 8, 9, 9 Pro and fēnix E; Enduro and Enduro 3; MARQ (Gen 1 and Gen 2); D2 Mach; Descent MK2, MK3 and G2. The Connect IQ Store shows the exact list for your model.
