## Layout

- **Full view (one move):** a horizon line and today's sun path (sunrise left, sunset right; one shared time axis) in the
  track grey. A dashed 45-degree sill line in muted. The part of the path above the sill (the window) is drawn three times the
  line weight in the accent. The sun disc sits at "now" on the path: **filled = OPEN, outline = CLOSED** (before, after, or
  demoted by the sky). On a NONE TODAY day the whole path stays under the sill, so the picture says it without a word.
- Under the horizon, centred: the state word (`OPEN`, `CLOSED`, `NONE TODAY`), then one reason line (muted) when there is
  one ("Opens 10:26", "Cloud cover", "Closed for today", "Sun stays low", "No weather"), then today's times `10:26–16:16` (muted;
  not on NONE TODAY).
- Proportions (fraction of screen height): horizon 0.49, 90 degrees = 0.40, path x from 0.11 to 0.89 of the width. Instinct:
  horizon 0.52, 90 degrees = 0.30, path x 0.12 to 0.62 (left of the sub-window).
- **Instinct sub-window:** shows the state mark (bar + disc), large, white.
- **Glance:** muted title "Sun Window" (XTINY), then the mark and the state word in `FONT_GLANCE`. No times (ADR-006). On
  Instinct E the title sits left of the sub-window and the state row under it (full 154/164 px width). No background fill
  (the system's themed card stays). First run: "Open once".
- Rows fit by shorter wording before a smaller font (owner 2026-10-03): "Sun stays low today" became "Sun stays low", "Open the
  app once" became "Open once".

## Typography

Fonts per the front matter; heights and widths measured, not taken from the SDK appendix. `FONT_GLANCE_NUMBER` and the
`FONT_NUMBER_*` fonts are digit fonts on several devices (letters measured implausibly), so no word uses them. Real Garmin glyph
shapes differ from the browser's; widths match.

## Iconography

Only drawn primitives: the sill bar, the disc (filled or 2 px+ outline), the path. The mark's stroke is at least 2 px so it
survives 1-bit. No icon font, no bitmap.

## Colour rule

**The accent marks nothing.** It colours the window segment and the disc in every state; it never changes with the state.
State is carried by the word and by fill/outline/position, so a greyscale or 1-bit render keeps every state (mockup section 3,
Instinct frames). The glance never uses the accent: its mark is white, so no accent is ever drawn on the themed card (up to
`#525252`), and Pink (2.53:1) and Violet (1.94:1) never sit on grey. On Instinct the accent menu is hidden (ADR-009).

Accent list (ADR-013, append-only ids): 0 Sky `#55AAFF` (default), 1 Mint `#55FFAA`, 2 Amber `#FFAA00`, 3 Pink `#FF55AA`,
4 Violet `#AA55FF`, 5 White `#FFFFFF`. Each is 64-colour-safe and at least 3:1 on black. The app has no always-on state, so the
dimmed-form rule does not apply. Reserved roles it must not equal: text `#FFFFFF` (White is allowed: it only colours the
window, which the outline and fill still distinguish), muted `#AAAAAA`, track `#555555`.

## Motion / always-on

None. An app has no always-on mode. The full view redraws once a minute (one timer, started in `onShow`, stopped in `onHide`).

## UX

- Glance budget: one word plus one mark, readable in under 2 s.
- Buttons only: START opens the app from the glance; in the app, START asks for a new fix in the no-fix state, MENU opens the
  accent list (ADR-009; D11 records the gesture). BACK leaves.
- Empty and error states, each a sentence: first run (glance "Open once", app "Finding your place"), no fix ("No place yet",
  "Press START"), no weather ("No weather" under OPEN, which is then from the sun's height alone), NONE TODAY ("Sun stays low").
- Permission: `Positioning` is asked by the system at install; the app explains it only on the support page and listing.

## Craft

The focal read is the state word under the picture of today's sun path; cut were the sun's height in degrees, any UV number,
a countdown, the next-open date on NONE TODAY days, and the accent on the glance.
