## Description

```text
Is the sun high right now? One word on your watch: OPEN, CLOSED or NONE TODAY.

The window
The window is open while the sun is at or above 45 degrees, the height where your shadow is no longer than you are. Open the app to see today's opening and closing times. On days when the sun never climbs that high, it says so.

The sky
On a day when your watch's own weather reports a low UV index, an open window shows as closed.

In your glance list
The state sits in your watch's glance list, so a scroll shows it without opening anything.

One setting
Accent colour, six to choose from, in Garmin Connect or in the app's menu.

For information only; not medical advice or a sun-safety tool.

If this app works for you, a rating in the store helps other people find it.

Location and privacy
Sun Window uses your watch's location to work out the sun's height. The place is rounded to about 11 km and stays on your watch. It reads your watch's own weather on the watch. No account, no internet, no analytics, no ads.

Support and answers: https://verden.watch/sun-window/support/
```
