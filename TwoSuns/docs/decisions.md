## ADR-022: Weather row in Pro

**Status: Proposed, BUILT (UNRELEASED). Owner overrode weather non-goal in chat on 2026-10-03, chose direction below and approved mockup's look same day (`docs/archive/weather-mockup.html`); built same day, simulator-grade evidence only; nothing uploaded. ADR stays Proposed with rest of Free + Pro pair (ADR-020).** Source: `../reports/Two Suns temperature research.md` (Rev 2, Rev 3) and `../research_notes/Two Suns temperature research/`.

**Decision.** Pro gets one new row, weather row, between time and Body Battery band. Free unchanged.
- **Now cell (only coloured part).** Condition icon and **one number, feels-like temperature** (`Weather.getCurrentConditions().feelsLikeTemperature`, SDK: "the wind chill or heat index", Celsius; converted for `System.getDeviceSettings().temperatureUnits`, shown as `15°`, no letter, no word). Actual temperature not shown. Icon colour fixed per icon type, never per value (studio rule: category-keyed colour allowed, value-keyed colour is verdict): sun and bolt `#FFFF55`, cloud and snow `#FFFFFF`, rain `#00AAFF`. **Number is one hue, `#FFFF55`, whatever the condition** (changed after design review: number taking icon's hue made storm number as cheerful as clear one and cloudy number white like time). Lead icon 1.4 times ahead icon (43 px against 31 on 454, as in approved mockup), except in stacked next-day cell, where weekday sits over ahead-sized icon. Both hexes 64-colour-safe; `#00AAFF` is 8.2 : 1 on black, computed.
- **Ahead cells (mono, `#AAAAAA`).** Up to three condition icons from `Weather.getHourlyForecast()` at even steps from now to sunset, hour under each in watch's 12 or 24 hour format. Icon and hour only: hourly forecast has condition, temperature, precipitation chance, wind, UV and cloud cover but **no per-hour feels-like** (SDK `HourlyForecast`), so temperature there would mix two quantities.
- **After sunset, and before sunrise:** row shows next daylight day from `Weather.getDailyForecast()`. **Only after sunset** it carries chevron and weekday; before sunrise it is today, which date row already names, so cell has no label. Label: chevron and weekday (from date words Pro date row already has; chevron marks next day, owner choice 2026-10-03, no new word to translate), condition icon, high and low, then up to three hourly icons only where hourly list reaches that day. No night forecast.
- **Seven icons** (clear, partly cloudy, cloudy, rain, storm, snow, fog) drawn from primitives, as `DESIGN.md` requires (no bitmaps). The 54 `Weather.CONDITION_*` values of SDK 9.2 (all since API 3.2.0) folded onto them in `TwoSunsWeatherKind`; windy and unknown, and any value newer than list, have no icon: hidden, never guessed. Grey (mono) partly-cloudy icon draws cloud over black knockout so sun and cloud stay apart.
- **Fit ladder (measured).** Row is full two-line row (icon plus hour label) where stack fits, otherwise **compact one-line row** height of date row (icon, number, three ahead icons, no hour labels), otherwise gone. When next-day row wider than 75% of chord, **low goes first**, then ahead cells (`TwoSunsWeatherRow.lowKept`, `weatherNextDayDropsTheLowWhenTheRowIsWide`). Order of giving way, **as built**: full row steps down to compact first, then date drops, then curve, then row, then sun line (ADR-016, rows follow font heights, amended). First draft of this ADR put date before compact; that would have dropped date on 218 px screen, which owner's approved mockup keeps. Spare height from `twoSunsLayoutReport` (container run, 2026-10-03; row heights from report, icon 16 px or larger, label as tall as date row). Rows as built are 68 px (454), 54 (360), 35 (218, 240) and 19 for compact row on 218; `weatherRowFormFollowsTheScreen` asserts compact with date on 218 px and full row with date on 240 px and larger:

  Not measured: 466 and 448 by 486 screens. `everyStateFitsThisDisplay` (every state, now four weather states) passes on devices listed under Evidence.
- **Empty and stale.** Read that fails or comes back empty keeps last good data (`TwoSunsWeatherSource`), so flaky read does not make row, and with it whole stack, jump; age rules below still drop what aged out. Remaining jump (time moves 37 px on 454 when row appears or goes) happens when setting turned off, when data aged out (3 hours) or never came, and after sunset when Garmin has no entry for tomorrow. No forecast, or forecast entries older than current hour: those cells not drawn. `observationTime` older than 3 hours (proposal): now cell not drawn. Nothing at all: row absent and face re-stacks to today's layout. Never invented or greyed value. If `feelsLikeTemperature` null but `temperature` not, proposal is show `temperature` (owner may prefer hiding it).
- **Not drawn always-on.** AMOLED always-on frame stays time, Body Battery value and sun sentence.
- **Setting.** One list setting `Weather` (Off, On), key appended, default On in Pro only. Shipped key never changes (house rule).
- **Code.** `Weather` stays un-imported and named in full inside `(:pro)` functions; `(:free)` twin returns null. No new permission (compiler: `Weather` needs none; Pro already calls `Toybox.Weather.getCurrentConditions()` in `TwoSunsSources`). Readings gathered in `TwoSunsSources`/`TwoSunsReadings`, drawn from `TwoSunsState` (house rules).
- **Accent roster.** Planned accent ids 8 (yellow `#FFFF55`) and 6 (cyan) must not be admitted while weather hues are `#FFFF55` and `#00AAFF`; test `noAccentIsAWeatherHue` goes with build. Default sky accent `#55AAFF` is one channel step from rain blue `#00AAFF`, and autumn `#FFAA00` from sun yellow. With number now one yellow hue, rain blue only appears on 2 px drop strokes inside white cloud, so confusion risk small; stays **look gate for a real screen** (accent test rejecting one-step neighbours would fail shipped default, so none added).

**Design review (watch-design-reviewer, fresh context, 2026-10-03; read from code and layout numbers, no pixels).** Applied: lead icon 1.4 times ahead icon (build had drawn them equal); 15 more conditions mapped; mono partly-cloudy knockout and lower bolt; thinner chevron; sun rays computed in one multiply; larger flakes; compact ahead icons row height (19, not 15); weather row re-measured after curve re-plan; number one hue; chevron only after sunset; low dropped first on wide row; last good data held. **Not applied, owner's call:** reserving row's height so time never moves; dropping date row on 454 px screen whenever weather row is full (stack is 318 of 326 px). **Cannot be judged without a screen:** stroke rendering at 16 to 31 px, whether yellow pulls eye from white time, glare on MIP, real inputs.
**Why.** Owner wants current conditions and day's weather at a glance, daytime only. Hourly forecast reaches about 12 hours (forum, not in SDK docs, unverified on a watch), so covers most of day from mid-morning; early morning gets shorter strip, daily forecast covers next day. Coloured now cell with mono strip keeps one focal weather read and leaves time as hero.
**Alternatives.** Thermal ticks on ring (temperature trend, no conditions): demoted to optional later add-on. Hairline trace: rejected, wandered into ring in mockup. Numbers only in date row: kept as fallback shape if owner drops row. Actual plus feels-like temperature: owner chose feels-like only. Per-hour computed feels-like (wind chill or heat index from temperature, wind, humidity): not built, modelled value that could disagree with Garmin's own number.
**Built.** `TwoSunsWeather` (row's words, shared so `TwoSunsState` compiles in both tiers), `TwoSunsWeatherData`, `TwoSunsWeatherKind`, `TwoSunsWeatherPlan`, `TwoSunsWeatherIcons` and `TwoSunsWeatherRow` (all `(:pro)`), `TwoSunsFrame` (weather mode, step-down), `TwoSunsLayout.rows` and `stackHeight` (weather row argument), `TwoSunsSources.readWeather` (cached 5 minutes), setting `Weather` (`TwoSunsConfig.KEY_WEATHER`, Pro list setting, default On). 19 new Pro-only tests (Pro 154, Free 67 unchanged). `setting_weather` title in every language folder as **machine draft, unreviewed** (owner's call, publish-checklist F11).

## ADR-001: Concept

**Decision.** One face answers one question: how much light, and how much energy, do I have left today? 24-hour ring around bezel is sky's sun, 24-hour curve under time is watch's Body Battery, one sentence at bottom says how much daylight left or when sun returns. Time is hero.
**Why.** No rival relates the two: 282 of 1,378 surveyed faces mention both, almost all as 14-to-40-field dashboards; none built around the pair.
**Evidence.** Desk research: `reports/Body Battery and sun face research.md` §1 and §2. Build's look is spec's recommended direction and owner has **not** approved it ([`../DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md)).
**Reversed by.** Owner replacing look with mock-up (then `spec.md` and `DESIGN.md` change, concept stands), or decision that face should not carry Body Battery at all.

## ADR-003: Sunrise and sunset come from Complications

**Decision.** Today's sunrise and sunset are `Complications` SUNRISE and SUNSET (seconds since local midnight, 0 to 86399, truncated to minutes): Garmin's own numbers, ones watch's Sunrise/Sunset glance shows, in watch's local time, with no location. `Weather.getSunrise` and `getSunset` are **not** primary source; allowed only as cross-check and as tier B fallback (1.1), and never without a location. Code today does not call them at all.
**Why.** Reviewers compare face with Garmin's glance, and Complications need no location. Rivals' "it shows UTC" bugs not explained by Weather API (below), so no API defect to design around.
**Evidence.** Desk research plus simulator probes, `research_notes/Body Battery and sun face research/platform.md` §3 and `probe/sim-sun-date-sweep-hourly.log`. Hourly sweep (simulator's clock UTC+3) asked `Weather.getSunrise/getSunset` for every hour from 26 September 18:00Z to 27 September 02:00Z for Hawaii, Sydney and London. Returned pair flipped at 26 September 21:00Z, which is 00:00 on watch's clock, not at 00:00Z: API follows watch's **local** calendar day. **Earlier reading was wrong and is retracted:** first sweep in 6-hour steps spanned both local and UTC midnight and was read as "UTC date". Simulator only; whether real firmware behaves same is what on-watch probe log (an `A` line across local midnight) records. Complication values in simulator are canned (sunrise 54535, sunset 11599, sunset earlier than sunrise), so prove parsing and after-midnight rule, not Garmin's numbers.
**Reversed by.** Device compare: if Complication values differ from native glance by more than 1 minute, or are null on owner's watch, calculation becomes primary and this ADR superseded. If then no location, tier A has no sun data and owner decides ([`plan.md`](archive/plan.md) "Stop and ask").

## ADR-004: Own NOAA calculation, and tomorrow's sunrise

**Decision.** Everything sky needs that Garmin does not give is own calculation (`TwoSunsSun`, pure `Float` functions): sunrise, sunset, solar noon, civil twilight (zenith 96 degrees), golden hour (84 degrees), evaluated at local noon of local date, zenith 90.833 degrees for sunrise and sunset. After sunset face announces tomorrow's sunrise as calculated tomorrow plus (Garmin's today minus calculated today), so keeps Garmin's offset. No copy of any library: only public Monkey C one is LGPL.
**Why.** Garmin gives no tomorrow, dawn, dusk or golden hour. Correction keeps two sources consistent to the minute.
**Evidence.** Simulator test: `TwoSunsSunReferenceTest` (generated by `tools/gen_sun_tests.py` from `reference_sun_times.tsv`): 27 place-and-date reference tests against US Naval Observatory, tolerance 2 minutes where Observatory lists event (rise, set, civil twilight; solar noon compared around the clock). Polar rows assert "no event" and sun's kind; two polar-circle transition days (Tromso 18 May and day midnight sun starts) check solar noon only, so difference of one day at transitions accepted, as spec says. Maths runs on days since J2000 in 32-bit floats because Julian date does not fit `Float`. Not verified: that watch's float behaviour equals simulator's; calculation's agreement with Garmin's own numbers (simulator's `Weather` day was 7 minutes longer than Observatory's for London on 27 September, and which zenith Garmin uses undocumented).
**Reversed by.** Device compare showing our calculation and Garmin's differing by more than tolerance in way tomorrow correction does not absorb.

## ADR-005: Location order and the Positioning permission

**Decision.** Location sources read in this order: `Activity.getActivityInfo().currentLocation`, then Weather observation location, then `Position.getInfo()`, then saved place (ADR-006). Manifest declares `Positioning`, **now confirmed, not provisional.** `Position.getInfo()` isolated in one function, `TwoSunsSources.positionLocation`, and its one entry in `updatePlace()`, kept that way even though permission confirmed, so future reversal stays two-deletion change. Every location source guarded; watch without one gives null and face says "No place yet".
**Why.** On owner's FR965 (2026-09-27, real watch, not simulator): `Activity.currentLocation` stayed null even right after GPS run started and discarded (Q1 = no); Weather observation location stayed null throughout, with or without GPS activity (Q2 = no); `Position.getInfo()` returned location immediately at first look, with no GPS activity run first — cached fix (Q3 = yes). Per decision table (`plan.md` phase 1): Q1 and Q2 no, Q3 yes → keep Positioning. Simulator behaviour for Q3 was opposite (always null without activity); real watch differs.
**Evidence.** Simulator probes (`platform.md` §1 and §2) plus on-watch probes, run 2026-09-27 (`../../device-test/LocationProbe-P.prg` with permission, `LocationProbe-N.prg` without; results in `../../device-test/LocationProbe-RESULTS.md`). M1 (first look) and M2 (after GPS activity, discarded) run on N; M3 (outdoors, over time) and M4 (overnight log) not, nor native-glance comparison (Q4) — none can change this call (Q3 already answers it), so left for later, before store submission.
**Reversed by.** Nothing found so far reverses it. If later run of M3 or M4 ever shows Activity or Weather giving location without Positioning, drop permission, delete `positionLocation()` and its entry in `updatePlace()`, and say "no location permission" on listing.
**Amended 2026-10-01** (ADR-020, the Free + Pro ladder, accepted 2026-10-04): Positioning permission, `positionLocation` and whole location order are Pro only; Free manifest has `ComplicationSubscriber` alone and Free reads no location. Pro unchanged.


## ADR-006: The remembered place

**Decision.** Place is `[latitude, longitude]` rounded to 0.1 degree (about 11 km), kept in `Application.Storage` under key `place`, only thing app stores. Fresher source replaces it only when more than 0.1 degree away (plus 0.001 epsilon) in latitude or longitude; since both values already rounded, in practice a step of 0.2 degree, and jitter causes no writes. Fix of exactly (0, 0) rejected as no-fix placeholder, and so is anything outside +-90 or +-180. Values read back from Storage accepted as any numeric type (Float, Double or Number) and re-validated. Failing store only loses memory. Never sent anywhere.
**Why.** Sun times need minutes per quarter degree, so 0.1 degree enough and not precise location. Storing little, writing rarely and validating on read protects face from bad or foreign value.
**Evidence.** Simulator test: `TwoSunsPlaceTest` (rounding, source order, replace only when moved, empty, (0, 0), storage types). Nothing about real fix.
**Reversed by.** ADR-005 removing every location source that could fill it (then key unused), or listing/privacy decision that face must not store place.
**Amended 2026-10-01** (ADR-020, the Free + Pro ladder, accepted 2026-10-04): Pro only. Free build has no remembered place and uses no `Application.Storage` at all.


## ADR-007: Always-on

**Decision.** On AMOLED watches requiring burn-in protection sleep frame is time, Body Battery value and sun sentence, in dim grey, `#5C5C5C` (amended 2026-09-27 and 2026-10-04, below; ADR-027, always-on text is a dim grey), block stepping across 3 by 3 grid every minute; no ring, no curve, no glyph, no date. Time always two font sizes below whatever awake just picked, derived at draw time from awake's own choice (amended 2026-09-27, below) — not fixed size. MIP watches show full face at all times. Step is 35 permille of shorter screen side (`BURN_IN_STEP_PERMILLE`), 15 px on 454 by integer arithmetic.
**Why.** Commonly-cited version of Garmin's limit is at most 10% of pixels lit and none continuously for more than a few minutes; copied from Days To Go without primary citation in either app (see `knowledge/platform-facts.md` in `watch-design-kit`, tagged unverified). Moving block every minute keeps any one pixel in lit stroke for at most a minute either way.
**Evidence.** Underlying "10%" figure uncited, not Garmin-confirmed. Simulator test: always-on frame fits at every one of nine drift positions (`alwaysOnFrameFitsAtEveryDrift`). **Not measured:** lit-pixel share, ghosting, a night on real AMOLED.
**Reversed by.** Always-on night check ([`publish-checklist.md`](status.md) gate 5): if screen blanks or ghosts, raise `BURN_IN_STEP_PERMILLE` or shrink time, then repeat.
**Amended 2026-09-27** (`watch-design-reviewer`, `watch-design-kit`), two fixes:
1. Always-on text was `#555555`, 2.8:1 against true black — under this project's own ≥3:1 bar for persistent/always-on colour. Changed to `#5555AA` (3.3:1, same value as night track; safe to share because ring never draws during sleep). `TwoSunsPalette.SLEEP_TEXT`.
2. Sleep time font was fixed independent list (`SLEEP_TIME_FONTS`, starting at `FONT_NUMBER_MEDIUM`) that matched awake's font exactly whenever awake had already fallen back to `MEDIUM` — contradicting "two sizes smaller" on any screen where that happened. Now derived from awake's actual choice at draw time (`TwoSunsDraw.fontsBelow`), so genuinely two steps below awake on every screen, floored at smallest available font.
Also corrected code comments' claim that pixel stays lit for "3 minutes"/"three updates": re-deriving actual drift (`minute % BURN_IN_GRID`, `minute / BURN_IN_GRID % BURN_IN_GRID`) shows block's x-offset changes every single minute, so true dwell is one minute, not three — code already safer than documented, just documented wrong.

**Amended 2026-10-04 (ROADMAP 10.17, Garmin policies research; text above kept as written on 2026-09-26/27).** "10%" figure is **not uncited any more: Garmin publishes it**, and "Evidence" line above superseded on that point. Garmin's AMOLED FAQ ("How do I Make a Watch Face for AMOLED Products?", also in SDK docs, read 2026-10-04): burn-in protection runs when watch face is in foreground and system asleep, and "if more than 10% of the screen pixels are on or any pixel is on for longer than 3 minutes, the system will shut off the screen" (pixel is on in any colour but black). Caveats: (1) **Garmin's pages disagree on time**: that FAQ says 3 minutes, Entry Points page says "AMOLED always active (version 1)" stops "any pixel from being enabled for more than four minutes", or more than 10% of pixels; we design to shorter figure. (2) **Pixel rule is original Venu's.** Same FAQ: "Since the Venu 2, the rule for always-on is to use less than 10% of the screen's luminance", and Entry Points' "version 2" has only 10% pixel limit with no dwell time; which product uses which rule read at run time (`requiresBurnInProtection`, `System.getDisplayMode`), not from list we hold. So "lit-pixel share" is right measure only on original Venu; on Venu 2 and later it is luminance, where dim `#5555AA` counts for far less than white. (3) What remains unmeasured is this frame's own share: no heat-map run and no always-on night on a wrist (ROADMAP 3.1); simulator's File > View Screen Heat Map is Garmin's tool. Dwell finding above (one minute) holds against both 3 and 4 minute figure. Garmin's watch-face page also says avoid much white or blue: see finding in `DESIGN.md` under "Always-on (AMOLED)" (text colour, `#5555AA`).

**Amended 2026-10-05 (ADR-028, the rectangle track).** On rectangle always-on time is two steps below grown awake time in list that continues past `FONT_NUMBER_MILD` to `FONT_LARGE` and `FONT_MEDIUM`, so always smaller than awake. Round screens keep this ADR's rule.

## ADR-008: No verdicts on Body Battery

**Decision.** Body Battery shown as number and neutral picture: never mood, emoji, advice, threshold word or good/bad colour scheme (red/amber/green). Face never shows words good, low, rest or tired. Phone setting that hides curve labelled "Energy curve", not "Body Battery". "Body Battery" is Garmin's trademark: never in store name, icon or brand, used only descriptively in listing.
**Amended by ADR-023 (2026-10-03): pill is now bolt gauge** (same colours and same hollow-when-stale rule).
**Amended 2026-09-27 (owner): value, pill and current-point dot dim below 30** (`TwoSunsConfig.BATTERY_LOW_THRESHOLD`, `TwoSunsReadings.batteryAccentFor`) — chosen accent turns to `TwoSunsPalette.dim(accent)`, exact colour sky ring already uses for daylight already gone, so "dim" means same thing everywhere on face. Real exception to "no colour by level," narrowly scoped: one brightness step, same hue throughout (never hue change, never red), no word, no icon, no threshold named anywhere in UI. Exists because flat accent colour created own problem (normal value read as "looks low" purely from colour's own connotation, amber especially); two brightness states, keyed to chosen hue, read as "less charge" without reading as "bad." Pill's fill length and curve's height already carry continuous level signal; this only adds glance-level cue, and stops at two states because third palette-safe step collapses most accents into same grey already used for "stale," which would make "very low" and "no data" indistinguishable — rejected for that reason ([`DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md) "Energy curve, glyph and value").
**Amended 2026-10-08 (owner decision, 2026-10-08; ROADMAP 13.25 follow-up): dim below 30 is removed; 2026-09-27 amendment above reversed and kept as history.** Number, bolt and curve's current-point dot are accent whatever the level (`TwoSunsReadings.batteryColor`; `BATTERY_LOW_THRESHOLD` and `batteryAccentFor` gone). Two brightness states of one hue still told wearer "you are low" at threshold face chose, which is verdict this ADR forbids, only quieter. Problem amendment solved (flat amber accent reading as "low" on normal value) was solved again on 2026-09-27 by moving default accent to sky blue and naming amber "Autumn" (`TwoSunsPalette.ACCENTS`), so dim no longer pays for itself. **Evidence.** Desk research (`rival_reviews.md`, `garmin_sources.md`, `naming.md`). No test enforces absence of verdict WORD; `strings.xml` has no such words (checked by reading, and by `tools/check_strings.py` only for length). Suites PASSED 2026-10-08 in container on changed code: Pro 165 / Free 76 on `fr965`, `fr255s`, `venusq2` and `venux1`, 157 / 69 on `instincte40mm` (simulator only). ## ADR-011: The 24-hour ring is wall-clock

**Decision.** Ring shows local wall-clock minutes 0 to 1440, noon at top by default (setting puts midnight at top), clockwise. One degree is four minutes (marker and tick precision about +-1 degree). On daylight-saving change day ring has one-hour jump; documented, not fixed.
**Why.** Reads like clock and matches numbers on face. Alternative (elapsed time) needs zone database watch face does not have.
**Evidence.** Simulator test: `TwoSunsRingPlanTest` (angles always in range, arcs, ticks, both orientations). Sun tests cover London DST change days (29 March and 25 October) and New York 8 March, but that tests sun times, not drawn ring across change.
**Reversed by.** Device wear day across DST change showing confusing ring.

## ADR-012: The local UTC offset is derived exactly

**Decision.** Offset is `(local day number - UTC day number) * 1440 + local minute - UTC minute` (`TwoSunsLocalTime.offsetBetween`), from one instant read twice with `Gregorian.info` and `Gregorian.utcInfo`. Not wrapped to +-12 hours, so +14:00 works, and `System.getClockTime().timeZoneOffset` and `.dst` not used.
**Why.** Garmin docs say only "time offset from UTC in seconds" and "daylight savings time offset"; comparing two readings of same instant needs no assumption. Comparing day numbers as well as clock times keeps +14:00 and -10:00 (same clock time, 24 hours apart) from being mixed up. Replaces spec's original "wrapped to +-12 h" rule.
**Evidence.** Simulator test: `TwoSunsLocalTimeTest`. Probe Q8 on a watch (does `timeZoneOffset` plus `dst` equal derived difference?) not run and not needed for face.
**Reversed by.** Nothing planned; a watch where two readings of one instant disagree in way arithmetic cannot absorb.

## ADR-013: Sky states and the calculation fallback

**Decision.** `TwoSunsSky.resolve` takes Garmin's sunrise and sunset first. If both null but place known, calculation fills in; polar day or night comes from calculation only. If place unknown, sentence is "No place yet" when Complications exist and "No sun data" when they do not. If only one null (transition day) missing time taken from calculation when there is one. Sunset earlier than sunrise means sunset after local midnight (add 1440). After sunset sentence announces tomorrow's sunrise per ADR-004; with no place shows today's Garmin sunrise flagged as estimate ("Sunrise ~06:41"); if calculation says sun does not rise tomorrow says "No sunrise tomorrow"; if nothing known says "Sunset HH:MM" or "No sun data". **Known edge:** on day whose sunset is after local midnight (Reykjavik in June), minutes from 00:00 to that sunset read "before sunrise", because Garmin's pair describes date that is starting, not sun still up from yesterday.
**Why.** Every failure has sentence, never blank (category's signature complaint is blank sun times). Edge accepted because fixing it needs yesterday's pair.
**Evidence.** Simulator test: `TwoSunsSkyTest` (13 tests: boundaries, tomorrow's offset, estimate, after-midnight sunset, polar states, transition day, no place and no data, fallback to calculation), `TwoSunsReadingsTest`. Polar and transition rows use simulator's canned Complication values and calculation only.
**Reversed by.** Device compare; real Reykjavik-like day showing edge worse than expected.
**Amended 2026-10-01** (ADR-020, the Free + Pro ladder, accepted 2026-10-04): Free build never has place, so never says "No place yet": Garmin's null pair is "No sun data" (`TwoSunsSky.noCalculationState`, `(:free)` twin). Polar day and night, twilight and tomorrow's own sunrise need calculation and so are Pro only; Free after sunset shows today's Garmin sunrise flagged as estimate ("Sunrise ~06:41"), the no-place wording of this ADR.

## ADR-015: Body Battery data rules

**Decision.** Last 24 hours of `SensorHistory` Body Battery go into 96 buckets of 15 minutes by each sample's own `when` (never `getOldestSampleTime` or `getNewestSampleTime`); newest sample in bucket wins; only 0 to 100 valid (127 means not worn and is dropped, so are null and negative values). Sample stamped up to 5 minutes ahead is clock skew and kept in last bucket; later dropped. Value is **stale** when newest sample older than 60 minutes: curve, value and glyph muted, dot and glyph become outlines. History re-read at most every 300 seconds. Gaps stay gaps: no interpolation. If watch has no history API (or reading it throws), Complication BODY_BATTERY number used when valid, with no curve. **If history exists but has no valid sample, value is "--" and Garmin's number is not substituted.**
**Why.** Forum reports say oldest and newest sample times do not match samples. Wrong number worse than "--". Staleness is shape as well as colour (ADR-017).
**Evidence.** Simulator test: `TwoSunsBatteryTest` (11 tests: 127, negative, 101, null, empty, one sample, hours-long gaps, out-of-order, stale, skew), `TwoSunsReadingsTest`, `TwoSunsCurveTest`. Simulator's history is synthetic (measured 2026-10-08 on fr965 in container: 480 samples a minute apart, oldest first, first at clock and rest up to 8 hours in future, so face keeps one sample, one bucket, and draws no line), so real cadence, order and sample count unknown until probe log.
**Reversed by.** Probe Q6 and Q7: fewer than 20 samples in 24 hours means 1-hour buckets ([`plan.md`](archive/plan.md) phase 1). **Added 2026-10-08:** typical gap between consecutive samples over 15 minutes also matters, even with more than 20 samples a day: ADR-028's (the rectangle track) lone-dot rule draws curve only where two neighbouring 15-minute buckets hold samples, so such cadence would draw it rarely or never. Probe must report how often consecutive samples land in neighbouring buckets (gap distribution), not only n and smallest gap.
**Amended 2026-10-01** (ADR-020, the Free + Pro ladder, accepted 2026-10-04): in Free only Complication path exists (no `SensorHistory` permission, no curve, no stale state): see ADR-021 (Body Battery in Free). Pro unchanged.


**Amended 2026-10-10 (memory, not the rules).** History is streamed: each sample goes straight into its bucket (`TwoSunsBattery.add`, then `finish`), so read never holds raw samples on top of iterator, and old curve released before re-read. Bucket rules unchanged (`build` still feeds tests through same `add`). Why: ADR-024's 2026-10-10 amendment (Out Of Memory on the Instinct). Not done: incremental re-read of only newest minutes (20-minute read costs about 0.46 kB against 4.2 kB in simulator) needs bucket grid aligned to quarter hour, change to this ADR's 24-hour window; only if Instinct runs short again.

Proportional like every cap here, so scales same way on all 69 products; screen too small for it already falls back through `TIME_FONTS`. **Not yet re-confirmed by fit sweep or new device photo** — do before submit gate closes.

**Amended 2026-10-05 (ADR-028, the rectangle track).** On rectangle rows fitted to rounded box inside track instead of round chord, may use box's whole height, spread with equal gaps (`TwoSunsRectSpread`), and time grows into what box leaves (`TwoSunsRectFit`); drop order unchanged. Rectangles since looked at in simulator screenshots (not on a wrist).

## ADR-017: Ring, curve and glyph encodings

**Decision.** Ring: night track all the way round (`#5555AA`, 3.3:1 against black); civil twilight `#AAAAFF`; daylight still to come in accent, daylight already gone in dimmed accent (each `FF` channel to `AA`, nudged one further step when that would land on `MUTED` — amended 2026-09-27, below); golden hour `#FF5500` only when setting on and place known; white ticks at sunrise and sunset; sun marker at now, white with black halo (halo now wider than dot itself — amended 2026-09-27, below), **solid when sun is up, outline when not**. No place or no data: plain track and no marker. Midnight sun: fully lit. Polar night: dim, with twilight if it can be calculated. Curve: fill (`#5555AA` fresh, `#555555` stale), white line (muted when stale), newest point solid accent dot (fresh) or outline dot (stale). Glyph: level pill, not battery-shaped (no nub — amended 2026-09-27, owner feedback on real-device photo: battery-shaped glyph reads as watch battery on Garmin face regardless of fill colour), plain rounded bar filled left to right to level in accent, square-edged fill not rounded to match outline (amended 2026-09-27, below), hollow when stale or when value is "--". So stale is shape as well as colour, and state never colour alone.
**Why.** In bright sun dim tracks must still read: night track at least 3:1 against black, and every dimmed accent at least 4.6:1 (unit test `dimPartsStayReadable` asserts at least 3:1 for night track and for dimmed form of all six). One more colour is dimmer: stale curve fill is `#555555`, 2.8:1, awake-only, carried by shape and words rather than contrast.
**Evidence.** Simulator test: `TwoSunsRingPlanTest`, `TwoSunsCurveTest`, palette contrast test, `dimNeverEqualsMuted` (new). Contrast ratios computed from hex values, not measured on screen. **Look has not been approved by owner and no screenshot of face exists** (true when written, 2026-09-27; simulator screenshots exist since 2026-10-04, `../../docker/SIMULATOR.md`, and rectangles' look approved 2026-10-08, ADR-028).

## ADR-027: Always-on text is a dim grey, `#5C5C5C`
